# Code of Conduct

WikiFrames is made for the Wikimedia movement and every community that shares
knowledge in the open. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests,
discussions and events — is expected to help keep it welcoming.

We follow the spirit of the Wikimedia
[Universal Code of Conduct](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Universal_Code_of_Conduct):

- **Be respectful and civil.** Assume good faith, disagree about ideas rather
  than people, and help newcomers find their feet.
- **Be inclusive.** People join from every country, language, background and
  level of experience. Write plainly; avoid jargon where you can.
- **No harassment.** Insults, discrimination, unwanted attention, sharing
  someone's private information, or sexualised content are never acceptable.
- **Protect participants.** Never post real participants' photos, campaign
  links or deletion links in public.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately
through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Zaidusyy/WikiFrames/security/advisories/new)
(it is private, and not only for security issues). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation of anyone who does not follow this code.
